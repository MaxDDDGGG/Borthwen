What's Nearby

Explore the breathtaking walks, historic castles, and premier outdoor adventures of southern Snowdonia right from our doorstep.

Mawddach Trail

1/4 mile

The Mawddach Trail runs about 8 miles through to Barmouth, boasting gorgeous scenery along the way. The flat path is ideal for walking or cycling.

Walking & Cycling Route

Precipice Walk

3.1 miles

One of the most popular walks in southern Snowdonia, offering stunning 360-degree views from heights between 700-850 feet, looking down on the Mawddach River valley and Llyn Cynwch Lake.

Scenic Walking Trail

Cymer Abbey

2 miles

Foundered in 1158-9 and dedicated to the Virgin Mary, Cymer Abbey is a ruined Cistercian Abbey situated near the village of Llanelltyd, just north of Dolgellau.

Historic Landmark

Cadair Idris

7.5 miles

With three routes ranging 9-10km, Cadair Idris is the pride and joy of Dolgellau. Standing at 2,930ft with 5-6 hour treks, it's a beast of a mountain for experienced hikers.

Advanced Hiking

Coed y Brenin

7 miles

Explore beautiful walks and waterfalls between old gold mine ruins. A premier destination for mountain bikers with manmade single tracks and dual slalom courses.

Mountain Biking & Forest Walks

Barmouth Beach & Harbour

9 miles

A beautiful Blue Flag beach with sand dunes and crystal-clear water, alongside a charming harbour town featuring pubs, cafes, paddle boarding, and boat tours.

Coastal Town & Beach

Harlech Castle

20.6 miles

Constructed atop a spur of rock close to Cardigan Bay, this magnificent fortress was built by Edward I during his Welsh campaign between 1282 and 1289.

Historic Castle

Caernarfon Castle

43.2 miles

Built in the 1280s, Caernarfon Castle was the most expensive and elaborate fortress built by Edward I, renowned for its distinctive polygonal towers.

World Heritage Site

Bala Golf Course

18.2 miles

Take on a unique challenge with this 10-hole course set roughly 1,000 feet above sea level, offering incredible panoramic views of Lake Bala and surrounding peaks.
